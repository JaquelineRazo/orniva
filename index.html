FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a website take?

One week from brief sign-off for a Launch website. Larger projects get a timeline in the quote.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, content and website are yours.

Do I own the customer data?

Yes. Every contact your lead magnets capture belongs to you, stored with consent in your CRM.

Can you work with my current CRM?

Yes, if it fits. If not, we'll recommend and set up one that does.

Is your content made with AI?

Some of it. ORNIVA Content Studio combines creative direction with AI-assisted production for images, video and voice, and every piece is reviewed by a person before it goes out.

What's included in the monthly plan?

Hosting, updates, small copy changes and a monthly report. Anything beyond that is quoted by the hour.

Do you work outside the U.S.?

Yes. We focus on U.S. businesses and work with clients in other countries too.
